Pony Swim & Island Events

Witness the world-famous Chincoteague Pony Swim and catch Wallops Island rocket launches from the water.

Annual Tradition

The Chincoteague Pony Swim

Every July, the famous Chincoteague wild ponies swim across the channel from Assateague Island — a tradition that has captivated visitors for nearly a century. It's one of the most iconic wildlife events on the East Coast.

Captain Randy offers the best vantage point on the water. Watch from the comfort of the boat as the “Saltwater Cowboys” guide the herd across the channel. You'll be closer to the action than anyone on shore.

The Pony Swim takes place the last Wednesday of July each year. Book early — these tours sell out months in advance.

Special Events

Wallops Island Rocket Launches

Chincoteague Island sits just minutes from NASA's Wallops Flight Facility, one of the oldest launch sites in the United States. When rockets go up, Captain Randy takes you to the water for a front-row view you won't find anywhere else.

Feel the rumble of a rocket launch from the open water while surrounded by the beauty of Chincoteague's back bays. These special event tours are announced as launch dates are confirmed by NASA.
